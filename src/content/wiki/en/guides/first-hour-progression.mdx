A good first session in **Steal a Superpower** is about getting **combat progression and base income running together**. The developer confirms both systems: ability cards power your attacks, while cards placed on holders earn Cash even during fights.

This original walkthrough is a **decision-based checklist**, not a promise that every player will clear the same zone within an hour. The mechanics come from the [official Roblox game description](https://www.roblox.com/games/74183246285569/Steal-a-Superpower); card examples come from player-supplied in-game screenshots.

## Your first five decisions

| If you are wondering… | Do this next | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What should I open first? | Inspect available ability packs and the cards you already own | Every card has different moves |
| Which ability should I equip? | Compare the regular move, special move and level of your available cards | Card leveling unlocks stronger abilities |
| How do I earn Cash while fighting? | Put an eligible card on a base holder | The developer confirms holders produce Cash during combat |
| What unlocks the next zone? | Defeat the area boss | Boss victories open the next area and provide loot chests |
| Where should I spend upgrades? | Check whether damage, survivability or movement is limiting you | Power, Health and speed all have upgrade systems |

## Start with one usable card

You do not need the rarest card to **begin learning the controls**. The first objective is to know what your equipped ability does. Check its ability description, then see whether its strongest move is locked behind card levels.

For reference, these cards have been documented directly from gameplay screenshots:

- [Puncher](/powers/puncher/): Common; Power Jab and Mega Punch; displayed **$5/s**.
- [Brawler](/powers/brawler/): Common; Headbutt and Body Slam; displayed **$6/s**.
- [Hothead](/powers/hothead/): Uncommon; Fire Punch and Inferno Uppercut; displayed **$15/s**.

These displayed incomes are card-specific observations, not a complete ranking of combat effectiveness. Browse the [six-card verified index](/powers/verified-card-index/) for the current reference set.

## Make the base earn while you explore

The official game description says cards placed on holders make Cash even while you are fighting. Before an extended boss or zone session, **check whether your available holders are occupied**.

This does not imply that every card can be placed on every holder, that earnings are available offline, or that listed $/s values are identical at every level. Those details are not yet confirmed by the evidence reviewed here.

See [Base Cash Explained](/base/how-base-cash-works/).

## Treat a failed boss attempt as information

When a boss stops your progress, avoid automatically buying the next available item. Identify the bottleneck first:

1. **You cannot deal enough damage:** review card level, equipped moves and Power upgrades.
2. **You get defeated too quickly:** review Health-related upgrades and any clearly described temporary effects.
3. **Your journey between fights feels slow:** check the movement upgrades and trails offered in your version of the game.
4. **You cannot afford changes:** return to your base holders and work on Cash generation before another attempt.

These are sensible troubleshooting choices based on confirmed systems, **not boss-specific damage thresholds**. The developers publicly identify **Dominus Empyreus** as the final boss, but do not publish verified health values or a complete zone order in the game description.

## Collect rewards before moving on

After a boss defeat, the official description says to open the loot chest and collect the packs. New cards may change both your combat options and your base economy. It is worth checking your collection before rushing into the following zone.

The game's **Index** also provides permanent collection rewards by rarity. Your collection can matter beyond the card currently equipped.

## Common early mistakes to avoid

- **Ignoring holders:** you lose an opportunity to earn Cash during the same time you spend fighting.
- **Judging cards by displayed Cash alone:** moves and level matter too.
- **Assuming every special is immediately unlocked:** the developer explicitly connects powerful moves to leveling.
- **Treating third-party codes as guaranteed active:** check our [Codes reference](/codes/overview/) for official-announcement evidence and clearly labeled unverified entries.
- **Guessing what a new aura costs:** inspect the current in-game shop before spending.

## Next steps

If you can defeat the next boss, open the chest, inspect new cards and reconsider your upgrades. If you cannot, use the bottleneck table above instead of repeatedly attempting the same fight.
