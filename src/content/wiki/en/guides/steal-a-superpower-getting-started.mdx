# Steal a Superpower Beginner Guide

**Steal a Superpower** is a Roblox experience by **11 R$ Productions**. Its gameplay centers on collecting ability cards, battling through zones, defeating bosses, and growing a base that earns cash.

This guide covers the mechanics confirmed in the [official Roblox game description](https://www.roblox.com/games/74183246285569/Steal-a-Superpower). Precise costs, drop chances, and damage values have not been independently tested for this guide.

## 1. Open packs and collect powers

Open ability packs to receive superpower cards. The game describes power types including **lightning, fire, frost, gravity, time, shadow, and gold**.

Each card has its own set of moves. Rather than assuming every card plays the same way, check what your current card can do.

See the [Powers guide](/powers/how-superpower-cards-work/) for an explanation of card leveling and the collection system.

## 2. Level cards to unlock abilities

The official description says leveling a card unlocks its most powerful ability. That makes card progression important alongside acquiring more cards.

It does **not** provide a universal leveling cost or exact unlock level for every card, so those figures should be checked in the game.

## 3. Fight through zones and defeat bosses

Use your powers to battle enemies across the game's zones. Defeating a **Dominus boss** opens the next area. Bosses also provide loot chests and packs.

See [Dominus Bosses and Zones](/bosses/dominus-bosses-and-zones/) for the confirmed boss progression loop.

## 4. Set up your base income

The developer explains that placing cards on **holders** in your base makes them earn cash, including while you are fighting. This connects your collection to your economy.

Learn the system in [How Base Cash Works](/base/how-base-cash-works/).

## 5. Improve your stats

Upgrades, **trails**, and **auras** are described as ways to boost **Power, Health, and speed**. The game also includes kill-streak bonuses and critical hits.

Read [Upgrades, Auras and Trails](/upgrades/auras-trails-and-stats/) for the verified basics.

## 6. Collect Index rewards

Completing the **Index** provides permanent rewards for collecting rarities. The official description also mentions Top Cash and Top Power leaderboards.

To avoid relying on outdated information, check the in-game Index for current rarity requirements and rewards.

## Frequently asked questions

The official game description advertises a free **Legend Pack** for players who like the game and join the developer group. Check the current in-game or official instructions before claiming it.
