## How to redeem codes in Steal a Superpower

1. Open **Steal a Superpower** in Roblox.
2. Click the **Codes** button in the **top-right** area of the game screen.
3. Enter or paste a code **exactly as written**, including capitalization.
4. Press **Redeem** and check the result.

If a newly released code fails, try rejoining a fresh server once. A failure may also mean the code has expired or has already been redeemed on your account.
