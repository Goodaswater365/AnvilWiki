# Dominus Bosses and Zone Progression

## What do Dominus bosses do?

**Steal a Superpower** features combat zones guarded by bosses. According to the developer's official Roblox description, defeating a **Dominus boss** opens the next area.

Defeating bosses also gives access to **loot chests** and **packs**, so boss progression connects combat with card collection.

## How should beginners prepare?

The official description establishes two useful preparation systems:

- **Ability cards** have distinct moves and can be leveled to unlock their strongest ability.
- **Upgrades, auras, and trails** can improve Power, Health, and speed.

A practical approach is to explore your equipped moves, improve your card, and check your available stat upgrades before attempting the next boss. Specific boss strategies and thresholds still need playtesting.

## Who is the final boss?

The official game description explicitly names **Dominus Empyreus** as the final boss.

It does not confirm the boss's current HP, attack rotation, exact location, or loot odds in the public description. This page does not invent those details.

## What happens after beating a boss?

Boss wins can open the following zone, and bosses have chests and packs associated with them. The precise reward quantities and probabilities have not been independently checked.
