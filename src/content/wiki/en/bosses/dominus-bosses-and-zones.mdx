The main purpose of bosses in **Steal a Superpower** is progression. According to the developer, a successful boss battle **opens another area** and provides a **loot chest connected to ability packs**.

## A simple boss-readiness test

Before you spend resources on upgrades, use three questions to locate the problem:

**Can you deal meaningful damage?** If regular attacks are not making progress, inspect your card's regular ability, whether its special is unlocked, and the Power options currently available. Higher Cash income does not automatically prove that a card is a better fighter.

**Can you stay alive long enough?** If you are defeated before finishing a fight, compare your available Health-oriented options rather than focusing entirely on damage. The game description confirms upgrades to Health, but not an exact threshold for any particular boss.

**Can you afford another attempt or upgrade?** Cards on base holders generate Cash while you play. Check that system before a longer session. There is no need to leave it idle while trying bosses.

## What to do after a boss victory

Once a boss is defeated, inspect the **loot chest and packs** and then the new area. Review any obtained cards before deciding how to proceed. A newly acquired card could provide different moves or a different displayed base income.

Don't assume that a chest grants a guaranteed rarity. The developer confirms packs but does not specify drop chances in the public description.

## Dominus Empyreus: the final challenge

The developer names **Dominus Empyreus** as the final boss. The Roblox page also asks whether players can obtain an **Ancient** card and beat it. This phrasing promotes a goal; it does **not establish an Ancient card as a strict entry requirement**.

A complete boss-by-boss health and weakness table would need verifiable in-game evidence. We therefore avoid publishing imagined locations, phases, health, or recommended level numbers.

## Quick questions

### Is it worth upgrading before a boss?

If damage or survivability is holding you back, yes—compare relevant options. There is no verified universal purchasing order that works for every card and level.

### Are boss packs the same as purchased packs?

Both involve ability packs, but the public description does not document an exact one-to-one equivalence for individual pack types and rewards.

### How do I find the next area?

The advertised progression is to defeat the current boss. The official game listing does not provide a complete ordered zone map.
