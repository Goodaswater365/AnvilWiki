| Attribute | In-game value |

## Slugger vs. Puncher

Both [Slugger](/powers/slugger/) and [Puncher](/powers/puncher/) display **Common**, **$5/s** and **×1** in their screenshots. Their moves are different: Slugger uses **Bat Swing / Home Run Smash**, while Puncher uses **Power Jab / Mega Punch**. These facts alone do not determine which card is stronger in combat.
