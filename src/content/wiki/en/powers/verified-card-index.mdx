## Index and collection bonuses

One player screenshot displayed **1 / 62 found** in the game's Index, along with **Common 1/4** and **Uncommon 0/4** at the time it was taken. Those are a player's observed collection counts, not this site's number of documented entries.

The same Index interface showed an **Index Bonus** for Cash & Power and **+5%** indicators beside rarity rows. The exact unlock conditions should be verified in-game before treating those as a complete formula.

## How we verify entries

We only publish named cards and numerical details when we can read them in the actual game interface or confirm them through reliable official information. A player's screenshots may reflect a specific card level and game version; values may change.
