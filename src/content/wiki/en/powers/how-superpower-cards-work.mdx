## How do you get superpowers?

In **Steal a Superpower**, players open **ability packs** to collect cards that grant powers. The developer names these examples:

- Lightning
- Fire
- Frost
- Gravity
- Time
- Shadow
- Gold

This is a list of power **types mentioned in the official description**, not a complete database of individual cards or rarity tiers.

## Does every card have different moves?

The official Roblox description says **every card has its own set of moves**. It also confirms that a card's most powerful ability is unlocked by **leveling the card**.

Exactly how many moves each card has and the levels required are not specified in that source. Those details should be verified card by card.

## How do cards support progression?

Power cards serve more than one function:

1. Equip and use powers to fight across zones.
2. Level cards to unlock stronger abilities.
3. Place cards on **base holders** to generate cash.
4. Work toward the **Index** for permanent collection rewards.

The official game page confirms these systems, but it does not publish a reliable full list of card statistics or chances.

## What is the best power?

There is no independently verified, all-purpose best power on this wiki yet. A useful comparison would require in-game observations of moves, card level, availability, and combat performance.

Until that data exists, avoid ranking individual cards from unverified tier lists.
