It is easy to waste time on an upgrade that does not solve your immediate problem. In **Steal a Superpower**, the developer confirms upgrades, **auras** and **trails** that affect **Power, Health and speed**. The best next purchase depends on what is stopping your progress.

## Choose your next upgrade by your problem

| Current problem | What to inspect first | What to compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enemies take too long to beat | Power upgrades and card levels | Displayed gain versus available Cash |
| You lose fights before dealing enough damage | Health upgrades and aura effects | Survivability gain versus cost |
| Crossing zones takes too long | Speed improvements and trails | Movement improvement versus cost |
| You have a strong card but a locked special | Card level | Requirements for the next level |
| You cannot afford upgrades | Base holders and card income | Available holders and earnings |

This decision table is independent advice based on documented systems, not a claim that specific purchases have been play-tested as optimal.

## Card upgrades are different from auras

A card's own progression matters: the developer says **leveling a card unlocks its strongest ability**. An aura or trail should not be confused with upgrading a card's level.

For an example of a visible card upgrade, a player-supplied **Puncher** screenshot showed a **+1 LV** option costing **29** next to a purple resource icon, alongside **Fuse 1/5**. The resource name and complete mechanics were not clear enough to state confidently.

See [Puncher's documented screenshot details](/powers/puncher/).

## How to compare an aura or trail

Check four pieces of information in the current shop:

1. **Which stat changes?** Power, Health and speed solve different problems.
2. **Is it a multiplier or a flat increase?** These cannot be compared as if they use the same units.
3. **What does it cost?** A larger displayed number is not automatically the best value.
4. **Is it usable immediately?** Confirm any unlock or equip restriction first.

An independent player guide reports that auras give Power and Health multipliers and trails give speed bonuses. That is **secondary reporting**, not independently confirmed for every named item here. See the [public upgrades catalogue](https://bloxron.com/steal-a-superpower/upgrades) if you want to compare the reported item list; verify the current game shop before purchasing.

## Do effects stack?

**Not yet confirmed for individual aura, trail and potion combinations.** The developer also advertises kill-streak bonus multipliers and critical hits, but gives no universal stacking formula.

Do not assume that multiplying every displayed number together produces your final damage or income.

## Avoid upgrading blindly before bosses

When a boss is hard, first identify whether the limiting factor is damage, Health or movement. Then review one relevant upgrade type rather than buying everything available.

There is no proven universal 'best aura' or 'best trail' for every player, budget and game version.
