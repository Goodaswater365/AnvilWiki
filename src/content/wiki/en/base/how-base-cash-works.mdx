## What does the base do?

In **Steal a Superpower**, the developer describes a base mechanic where you **place ability cards on holders**. Cards on holders earn **cash**, including while your character is fighting.

This means cards are relevant to both combat and your base economy.

## The basic collection-to-income loop

1. Open ability packs to collect cards.
2. Use the cards' powers in combat.
3. Place cards on available base holders to generate cash.
4. Continue fighting and collecting while the base produces income.

This sequence describes the system documented on the official game page. It is not a claim that all cards have the same cash output.

## Which card should go on a holder?

The official description does not list individual card income rates, holder prices, or the number of available slots. Until those details are verified, this wiki cannot recommend a mathematically optimal holder setup.

Check each card's displayed income and any in-game holder restrictions before choosing a setup.

## Is the base a PvP stealing system?

The source verifies **card holders and cash generation**, but it does not establish any player-versus-player base raiding mechanic. Avoid assuming mechanics from other Roblox games with similar names.
