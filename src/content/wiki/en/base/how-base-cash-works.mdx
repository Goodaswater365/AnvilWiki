Your base is more than a place to store cards. The official game description confirms that **cards on holders earn Cash while you are fighting**. This allows collection and combat to progress alongside a separate income system.

## What is a holder?

A **holder** is a base location where a card can be placed to generate Cash. The developer confirms the system, but does not list every slot requirement, maximum capacity or offline-earning rule in the public description.

## A practical base-management routine

1. **Check your holders.** Make sure you are using any available card slots before starting a long fight session.
2. **Review card displays.** Compare cards you own rather than relying only on rarity names.
3. **Check any restrictions.** Some cards or slots may have requirements not documented in the public developer description.
4. **Continue progressing.** Boss fights and ability packs provide opportunities to expand your collection.
5. **Revisit your base.** After collecting new cards or leveling one, review your setup again.

This approach does not assume free placement, unlimited holders, stacking formulas, or offline Cash generation.

## Can you earn while fighting?

**Yes.** That is explicitly stated in the official Roblox game description. Whether income accumulates when the entire game is closed is **not established** by that description.

## Does the game's name mean PvP base theft?

The official description emphasizes opening packs, fighting through zones and placing cards on holders. It does not confirm a player-versus-player raid mechanic. Avoid treating assumptions from similarly named Roblox games as fact.
